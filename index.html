Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

Use the present simple for routines and facts; use the present continuous for actions happening now.

“I work every day.” · “I am working right now.”
Beginner
02
COMMON MISTAKES

Much vs. Many

Use many with countable nouns and much with uncountable nouns.

“How many books?” · “How much time?”
Beginner
03
ADVANCED

Although vs. Despite

Although is followed by a subject and verb. Despite is followed by a noun or gerund.

“Although it rained...” · “Despite the rain...”
বাংলা অর্থ: যদিও / তা সত্ত্বেও
Advanced
04
ADVANCED

Subtle vs. Delicate

Subtle means not obvious or easy to notice. Delicate means fragile, sensitive, or requiring care.

“A subtle difference.” · “A delicate situation.”
বাংলা অর্থ: সূক্ষ্ম / নাজুক
